# Datathon 2026 · Project Context

## Challenge
UMDAC Datathon 2026's preliminary-round case, supplied by Swift, asks teams to **reverse-engineer the rules and relationships used to generate an artificial cross-border payment dataset**. Results should be evidence-based; the dataset is synthetic and need not reproduce actual world-wide payment distributions.

## Official deliverables and timeline
- **Deadline:** 19 November 2026, 12:00 PM Malaysia time; no late amendments accepted.
- **Dashboards:** approximately 5–15 feature-group dashboards, submitted as PDF(s). Explain reconstructed rules and support each with relevant charts.
- **Presentation:** approximately 10–20 slides in PDF, including methodology, key findings, charts and economic interpretation. At most one summary slide for each feature group.
- **Video:** not required for the preliminary round.

## Overall judging rubric
- Insight quality: **45%**.
- Evidence, methodology & data integrity: **23%**.
- Communication: **20%**.
- Interpretation & economic reasoning: **12%**.

## Boundaries
- Analyse only the organiser-provided dataset as the *primary dataset*. External reference statistics may inform interpretation when appropriately cited in APA style with URLs.
- Do not disclose, publish or redistribute the dataset. Treat outputs derived from it carefully.
- AI assistance is allowed. Team members remain responsible for validity and explanation of methods and findings.
- Credit external tools and pre-existing code as required by the official documents.
