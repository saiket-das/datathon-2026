## Mechanism alternatives
- M0: independent uniform intermediary selection from a prespecified eligible bank universe.
- M1: global May marginal intermediary frequencies, with replacement conditional on route length.
- M2: May intermediary frequencies conditional on source/destination country pair and route length; back off only via a prespecified hierarchy.
- M3: path-template or sender/receiver bank conditioned sampling, with a finite set of repeated intermediary motifs.
- M4: sampling without replacement (or explicit within-route deduplication), which predicts suppressed repeated intermediaries even under concentrated marginals.
- M5: latent batch/time mixture with bank concentration shifts, capable of producing similar overall collision rates without the proposed country generator.

## Identifiability threats
The collision indicator must be defined before inspecting rows: repeated intermediate BIC8 within one route is not the same as shared intermediary between distinct routes, and repeated full path templates are not independent draws. Conditioning on path length is mandatory because opportunities for collisions scale with intermediate-node count. A single scalar collision rate cannot identify the sampling mechanism; compare conditional collision, concentration, route motifs and temporal drift on the same eligible records. Bank overlap, template reuse and shared country pairs induce dependence.

## G-032 prospective May-only estimator and fallback contract (design amendment, NOT a freeze)

For each eligible May route, count each observed intermediary **position**, including repeated positions, rather than one vote per unique bank or route. Define the candidate universe U solely from distinct intermediary BIC8 tokens in eligible May routes. No July token may enlarge U. The uniform M0 comparator uses U and abstains if U is empty; unseen July intermediary identities affect observed collision labels but never model weights. The estimator is conditional on May support, not a claim that the synthetic generator draws from a fixed universe.

Define an intermediary count k as the number of positions strictly between the two visible endpoints. Proposed M1 global weight for category b in U: (n_b + alpha)/(N + alpha*|U|), with alpha=1 fixed *before* reading May counts. For M2 use the same Dirichlet-one smoothing in each supported stratum, retaining the global U for every stratum, not a stratum-specific bank universe. A stratum is a **directed** visible-endpoint country pair and exact k. No July-derived country-pair, route-length or bank-frequency statistic enters fitting. Candidate country attribution must come from a prespecified static BIC-country mapping verified separately; if no reliable mapping exists, M2 is **unsupported**, not inferred from July or silently replaced with M1.

Proposed deterministic backoff for M2, assessed using May **eligible route counts** (not intermediary-position counts): directed country pair + exact k if at least 100 May routes; otherwise directed country pair pooled across k if at least 100; otherwise global exact k if at least 100; otherwise global M1. Empty U or unavailable required country mapping produces explicit abstention. The fallback level and number of routes at each level must be reported privately for both May and July; this hierarchy is fixed in advance as a *candidate* and cannot be described as validated or tuned. M0/M1/M2 must be compared on the identical common eligible July route set, with a separate model-specific coverage table.

**Key identifiability caveat:** M1/M2's May intermediary-position marginals are estimated from routes that may already contain motifs or deduplication, so fitting these weights does not prove iid sampling. Dirichlet-one smoothing across a large U may substantially dilute sparse strata. Predeclare sensitivity analyses for alpha=0.5 and 2 as secondary only; no selection by July score. Never use published B14 percentages to tune alpha or fallback support.

## G-033 prospective M3–M5 discriminators (design amendment, NOT frozen)

**M3: finite recurring-route motifs.** Define a motif as the ordered *intermediary-only* BIC8 tuple from a fully observed eligible May route, with the two endpoints omitted. Build May motif counts stratified by exact intermediary count k, and estimate the collision probability of an empirical motif draw using (colliding May motifs + 1)/(all eligible May motifs + 2) within k. This is a *collision-only* beta-one-smoothed template comparator, not a full-path likelihood. If fewer than 100 eligible May routes exist for k, back off to the global May eligible-route collision proportion with the same smoothing. Record May motif recurrence separately as a secondary explanatory statistic. Do not use July motif membership to select a model or to update probabilities. M3 can match collision rates while failing to predict new motifs; motif recurrence and collision forecasting must be reported separately.

**M4: strict without-replacement / deduplication null.** Conditional on any k <= the eligible intermediary universe size, strict unique selection predicts collision probability zero. For k greater than that size, strict without-replacement sampling is impossible and must be marked structurally unsupported, not scored as zero. For numerical Brier comparisons, use the exact zero probability; for logarithmic loss, apply one common fixed clipping epsilon=1e-6 to *every* candidate, not selectively to M4. Any observed eligible collision falsifies strict M4 on that route, subject to tokenizer correctness. A softened deduplication mechanism is a distinct future hypothesis, not a post hoc rescue.

**M5: temporal-mixture competitor.** Divide UTC May into fixed seven-day blocks starting May 1 (the final block is shorter); fit one May-only smoothed intermediary-position weight vector per block using the fixed May universe U. Compute block mixture weights as May eligible-route counts / total May eligible routes, never July-derived. For a July route with intermediary count k, M5's collision forecast is the mixture sum of block-specific exact iid collision probabilities at k. This is a latent *route-level* block mixture, not a mixture of each position independently; averaging block weights before calculating collisions would describe a different generator. Empty blocks have zero mixture weight; if May has no eligible routes, abstain. This candidate tests May heterogeneity, not actual July time adaptation. Fixed July UTC week bins beginning July 1 are for diagnostic calibration only, with no week-specific refitting.

**Common-score guard:** M0/M1/M3/M4/M5 are compared on the same parseable, uncensored, k>=2 July population whenever their May training support exists. M2 is included only when its external country map is verified; otherwise report M2 unsupported and do not shrink the common set for other models. Report each model's abstention count and the all-model intersection separately. For all candidates, compute route-level Brier and clipped log loss using the same observed binary within-route collision indicator. Do not declare an M3/M5 mechanism identified merely because its loss is lower: different generators can induce identical collision probabilities. Predeclare cluster bootstrap, thresholds and multiplicity before any validation; these candidate formulas are **not** a validation freeze.

**Potential falsifier:** If M3 and M5 have similar collision calibration but diverge on May-supported motif recurrence, the collision indicator alone cannot choose between them. Any claim of motif-based generation requires a separately frozen motif-recurrence validation on July; do not use the July motif-recurrence diagnostic to tune the collision models.

## G-034 prospective identifiability and inference protocol (2026-11-18; NOT FROZEN)

### Predeclared common population and observables

- Training UTC month: May 2023. Validation UTC month: July 2023; no June-based fitting, July support-based redefinition or use of previously exposed B14 numerical rates in model choice. All TRANSACTION_TYPE_CODE values remain in-scope for primary collision scoring; class strata are descriptive only.
- Parse BIC8_PATH using the *existing* strict G route contract, not a forgiving post-hoc parser. A route is primary eligible iff every token is a valid eight-character visible BIC, no ellipsis/censor, and there are at least two **intermediary positions** between the two endpoints. Endpoints are not scored as intermediaries. For each eligible route define k, ordered intermediary tuple T and binary C=1 iff T repeats a BIC. A malformed, censored or k<2 route is an explicitly counted unsupported stratum, **never** a zero-collision observation. Route parsing and B14 compatibility remain unverified against sponsor input.
- May-only intermediary-position universe U and weights use all **eligible** May intermediary positions, counting repeats. M0 uniform on U; M1 Dirichlet-one global on U; M2 same U and alpha with the already specified 100-May-route directed-country-pair/exact-k fallback. The model does not enlarge U using July tokens. For M2, a deterministic, versioned ISO country-code mapping from visible first/last BICs must be documented and schema-verified without July collision outcomes before freeze; otherwise M2 entirely abstains. Never backfill M2 from ord_country/ben_country or July country frequencies without an explicit new plan.
- M4 strict without-replacement predicts P(C=1)=0 for k<=|U| and abstains for k>|U|; a single correctly parsed C=1 is a counterexample to its universal claim, regardless of its Brier score. M5 uses G-033's five fixed May-week blocks (May 1–7, 8–14, 15–21, 22–28, 29–31), May eligible-route mixture weights and **blockwise** exact iid collision probabilities. It is supported only if at least two May blocks each contain 100 eligible routes; otherwise it abstains. All probabilities are computed before any July C is read.

### Genuine template-mixture competitor M3b (separate from M3a)

1. **Template dictionary:** eligible May routes dated May 1–21 UTC, grouped by exact k. A template is the full *ordered intermediary-only tuple*. Preserve May template multiplicities privately; print only aggregate counts. At least 100 early-May eligible routes of exact k are required, otherwise **abstain** on M3b for that k.
2. **Independent chronological tuning inside May:** for eligible May 22–31 routes of each k, require at least 100 late-May routes. Let S_k be early-May observed distinct templates. Define r_k as the fraction of late-May tuples in S_k. Under fixed May M1 weights p_b, let q_k = sum over distinct t in S_k of product over positions j of p_(t_j). This is the **iid M1 probability of any early-May template**, not a product of May collision rates. No July tuple enters S_k, r_k or q_k.
3. **Mixing estimate:** if 0<=q_k<1, lambda_k=clip((r_k-q_k)/(1-q_k),0,1). At q_k>=1 or insufficient May support, M3b abstains rather than divide by zero. For the copying component estimate early-May template collision probability a_k=(number of colliding early-May eligible routes of k+1)/(number of early-May eligible routes of k+2). Thus P_M3b(C=1 | k)=lambda_k*a_k + (1-lambda_k)*P_M1(C=1|k). This is a *finite copying versus iid mixture*, not a proof of copying. The estimator is trained entirely inside May.
4. **Separate July recurrence prediction:** under M3b, P(T in S_k | k)=lambda_k+(1-lambda_k)*q_k; under M1 it equals q_k. Evaluate actual July recurrence indicator 1[T in S_k] without updating S_k or using July to select k. Report recurrence Brier and log score separately from collision scores, with common M3b-supported cohort and unconditional / supported coverage. Require a substantive *out-of-period recurrence* improvement before describing the template mechanism as favored. Early-May finite motifs or deterministic identifiers must never appear in public outputs.
5. **Adversarial alternatives:** an unordered intermediary multiset cache and routing constraints may also yield recurrence. On early-May templates construct a deterministic within-route intermediate-position permutation placebo using a seed fixed at freeze; preserve bank multiset, k and C, but randomize order. Evaluate this placebo with identical late-May tuning and July recurrence, reporting both ordered and unordered recurrence rates. If ordering provides no discriminative gain, report a multiset/template ambiguity. M3a remains a simple May empirical by-k collision control, with 100-route fallback and beta-one smoothing as already documented.
